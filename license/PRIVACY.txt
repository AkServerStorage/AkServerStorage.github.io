# AkServer – Privacy Policy

**100% Offline • No Tracking • No Cloud • Open Source**

Last Updated: November 15, 2025

---

## Your Data Stays Local

AkServer runs **entirely on your device and local network**.

- **No data leaves your device**  
- **No usage tracking**  
- **No analytics**  
- **No cloud uploads**  
- **No accounts, emails, or personal info**

---

## What We Collect

**Nothing.**

- No device IDs  
- No timestamps  
- No screen resolution  
- No "adoption metrics"

---

## File Access

- Files are **never scanned, copied, or uploaded**  
- Only streamed **within your local network**

- Anyone can audit the code
